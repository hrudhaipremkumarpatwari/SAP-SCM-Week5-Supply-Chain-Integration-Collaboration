# Week 5 Internship Report: Supply Chain Integration and Collaboration in SAP SCM

This Week 5 report presents a Supply Chain Integration and Collaboration Analysis in SAP SCM using an illustrative case study for the fictional Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd., a manufacturer of 500-gram millet-based ready-to-cook food products. It examines how procurement, production planning, manufacturing execution, inventory management, quality, warehouse operations, transportation, sales, and external partners can share accurate information to support coordinated decisions. Public context is taken from APEDA’s Annual Administrative Report 2024–25, which reports growth in India’s cereal-preparation export category. That industry context is not treated as a forecast for the fictional company.

The report maps the roles of MM/procurement, PP/PP-DS planning, inventory, QM/quality status, SD/order and delivery management, EWM/warehouse execution, TM/transportation planning, and integration monitoring. It distinguishes legacy SAP APO integration through CIF from an SAP S/4HANA landscape in which PP/DS may be embedded, and emphasizes that configuration steps must be selected according to the actual SAP version and implementation scope.

A detailed workflow simulation follows a supplier’s 24-hour delay to a 62,000-piece packaging-material delivery. Using illustrative demand of 138.5 tonnes and an assumption of 2,000 pouches per tonne, it calculates a total requirement of 277,000 pouches. Opening stock of 215,000 pouches covers an equivalent 107.5 tonnes, leaving a temporary coverage gap equivalent to 31 tonnes while the delayed receipt is unavailable. The report shows how a purchasing update can trigger a planning review, a revised production sequence, stock and quality confirmation, warehouse readiness checks, transport rescheduling, and communication of at-risk customer dates.

Additional content includes an integration architecture diagram, end-to-end workflow, exception-resolution flow, KPI framework, proposed response targets, a cross-functional RACI matrix, risk and control register, twelve proposed validation tests, and a 30/60/90-day improvement roadmap. The emphasis is on practical governance: consistent master data, document references, timestamps, acknowledgements, error ownership, controlled retry, and end-to-end reconciliation.

All company-specific figures, events, response targets, and workflow outcomes are illustrative. The report is a learning simulation and configuration blueprint; it does not claim live execution or results from a production SAP system.
